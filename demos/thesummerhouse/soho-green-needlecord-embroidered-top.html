Appliqué work on the world’s finest 21 wales needlecord. Designed for tastefully done occasions. Can be worn front and back - choose between a V or modish neckline as desired. 

The garment is exquisitely embroidered by hand in our studio. Slight differences in embroidery speak to the hand process involved.

  • 100% cotton corduroy
  • Gentle machine wash with mild detergent
  • Do not bleach
  • Do not wring
  • Do not scrub
  • Wash with like colors
  • Ethically made in India
