    Size Guide Men

    Most pieces are cut loose, designed to layer and to soften with wear.

    Our sizes run from XS to XL. Samples are cut to a Medium and graded from there. Where possible, we'd love to make pieces in sizes outside this range. Most styles can be cut larger or smaller to your measurements through our made-to-order process, with a turnaround of seven to fifteen working days from the studio in Jaipur, after receiving measurements.

    If you sit between sizes, we usually recommend the smaller for a closer fit, the larger for an easier one. Exact measurements vary slightly by style.

    For made-to-measure or fit advice, write to studio@iroiro.in.

Flatweave crafted entirely by hand for a crisp washi-like texture.

    Handwoven

    Woven by hand on traditional looms, where every movement is guided by the weaver. The texture, rhythm and subtle irregularities of the cloth reflect the presence of the hand throughout its making.

    Made to Order

    Made only when requested, and crafted within our Jaipur studio. This considered approach allows each piece to be produced with care, avoiding excess while ensuring attention is given to every stage of its making.

    Soft Tailoring

    Structured with ease in mind, allowing the garment to move naturally with the body. Gentle shaping and thoughtful construction create a presence that feels assured rather than imposed.

    Flat Weave

    A technique requiring exceptional control and patience at the loom. The fabric is woven with a flat surface, demanding precise handling of every weft, before the woven waste strip is carefully secured by hand.
